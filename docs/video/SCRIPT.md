# EggFarm Legends — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** about 90 seconds (the 60-second cut is at the bottom)
**Format:** screen recording of the app (mobile or web) with voiceover, plus title cards
**Tone:** warm, playful, confident. It's a farm game first and a blockchain game second.
**Music:** light upbeat acoustic or chiptune, ducked under the voiceover

Before recording:
- Contracts are deployed to Arbitrum Sepolia.
- The wallet holds about 0.5 test ARB.
- Have one creature that's already mature and happy (≥80).
- Have one egg that's ready to hatch (sped up or pre-aged).
- Have a second creature ready for the breeding shot.

## Production notes

- **Resolution:** record mobile at 1080×1920 and web at 1920×1080. Frame the mobile capture
  inside a phone mockup for the landscape master.
- **Wallet popups:** keep them on screen for about one second so viewers can see the transaction
  is real, then speed-ramp past the confirmation wait.
- **Waiting periods** (hatch timers, cooldowns): don't fake them. Use pre-staged assets and say
  so in the description ("egg pre-incubated for the demo").
- **Captions:** burn in subtitles. Most viewers will watch on mute.
- **Show the chain:** in one shot, cut briefly to the transaction on Arbiscan (Sepolia) to prove
  it happened on-chain.
